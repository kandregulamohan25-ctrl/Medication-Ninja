What is Medication Ninja?

Medication Ninja is a free, gamified health companion app that helps you track your antibiotic and medication usage, monitor your personal antimicrobial resistance (AMR) risk, and learn how to use medicines safely. Whether you are managing a current antibiotic course or want to understand your long-term medication history, Medication Ninja gives you a clear, visual picture of your health.

Antibiotic resistance is one of the biggest global health threats today. When antibiotics are overused or taken incorrectly, bacteria evolve to resist them — making infections harder and sometimes impossible to treat. Medication Ninja helps you do your part to stop this by tracking your courses and educating you on safe practices.

Why Antibiotic Resistance Matters

According to the World Health Organization (WHO), antimicrobial resistance is one of the top global public health threats. Every year, at least 1.27 million deaths are directly attributed to antibiotic-resistant infections worldwide.

  • Over-prescribing — antibiotics prescribed for viral infections that don't need them
  • Incomplete courses — stopping antibiotics early allows resistant bacteria to survive
  • Self-medication — taking leftover antibiotics without a prescription
  • Sharing antibiotics — using someone else's prescription medicines

Medication Ninja educates and empowers users to make smarter decisions about antibiotic use, reducing their personal resistance risk and contributing to global AMR prevention.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Medication Ninja free?

Yes, completely free. No subscription, no hidden charges.

Do I need to install anything?

No installation required — it works in any browser. You can also install it as a PWA on your phone for offline access.

Is my health data safe?

Your data is stored securely and only accessible to you. We use Supabase with row-level security to protect your information.

Can I use this to replace my doctor?

No. Medication Ninja is an educational tool to help you track and understand your medication usage. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.

What antibiotics can I track?

You can track any antibiotic — Amoxicillin, Ciprofloxacin, Azithromycin, Augmentin, and more. The app identifies them and calculates your resistance risk accordingly.
